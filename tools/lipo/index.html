Flight time

Or estimate hover time from the weight of the whole aircraft and how many grams of thrust it makes per watt.

Both are rough estimates. Wind, throttle and battery age change real flight time a lot.

Charging

1C is the safe default. Only charge faster if the battery says it supports it.

State of charge (approximate)

Measure after the pack has rested for at least 30 minutes with nothing connected. Curves vary between cells, so treat this as a guide.

Safety

Charge with a balance charger on a non-flammable surface and never leave charging packs unattended. Don't discharge below the landing voltage under load. Store packs at storage voltage. Stop using any pack that is puffed, damaged or gets hot.
